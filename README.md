# Privacy Policy for POP

**Last Updated:** [Insert Date, e.g., June 2026]

POP ("the App") is a premium sensory relaxation platform designed to provide calming tactile micro-interactions through soothing visuals, audio, and haptics. We are deeply committed to protecting our users' privacy. 

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data within the App. By downloading and using POP, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

#### A. Personal Data
POP is built entirely with a local-first philosophy to ensure a peaceful, uninterrupted mindfulness experience. 
* **No Accounts Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.
* **No Data Collection:** We do not collect, harvest, track, or store any personal data, including but not limited to names, email addresses, phone numbers, or device locations.

#### B. Gameplay and Settings Data
Any data generated within the App (such as your unlocked themes, customized sound packs, high scores, or time-of-calm statistics) is saved exclusively on your physical device using secure local storage. This data never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

---

### 2. Permissions Required
The App requests only the native device permissions strictly necessary to deliver a premium tactile sensory feedback loop:
* **Haptics/Vibration:** Used solely to generate tactile physical vibrations when interaction loops (bubble popping) occur.
* **Audio:** Used solely to play soothing ambient backgrounds and satisfying acoustic click/pop sounds.

No permissions related to your identity, contacts, files, or location are requested or used.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services and Future Monetization
Currently, the core experience of POP is completely local. If future versions of the App introduce monetization features—such as rewarded ads, interstitial optional unlocks, or premium content purchases via the Google Play Store—those transactions and ad placements will be handled securely through Google Play Services. 

Google Play Services may collect certain diagnostic or device identifiers according to their own privacy frameworks. We recommend reviewing their policy:
* [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because POP does not collect any personal data or transmission tracking, it is naturally safe for users of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from anyone.
